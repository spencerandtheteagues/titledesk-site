Pricing

Pay for a run, or pay for a month.

A run is paid for before it starts: by card at checkout, or from a plan billed in advance. The check before payment is free, and so is your first upload run, up to 150 pages. If a run cannot be finished, a run is credited back to your account.

Every plan offers two document options at the same subscription price: copies billed to your saved card at cost, or copy costs included within hard per-run and monthly caps with a $1 fee per purchased recorded instrument. Multi-page instruments count once. Monthly copy pools are shared across the team and do not carry over. Included purchasing stops at either cap; there is no automatic at-cost overage. Solo and Pro retrieval adds $49 per run; Enterprise has no retrieval fee. The $60 Hybrid and $125 Full service allowances belong to individually purchased runs. Retrieval remains by invitation. Annual allowances refresh on each monthly anniversary. Manage renewal and your card through your billing portal.

Every run includes
