Verify what you downloaded

Every build has a published SHA-256. Check it before you open the image; a mismatch means the download is not ours.

macOS:    shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/TitleDesk-Agent-0.2.13-macOS-Apple-Silicon.dmg
Windows:  Get-FileHash .\TitleDesk-Agent-0.2.13-Windows-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
Linux:    sha256sum ~/Downloads/TitleDesk-Agent-0.2.13-Linux.AppImage

Start your first assignment

  1. Install TitleDesk and enter the activation code from your email.
  2. Open Project Run and complete the CountyRecords, report template, Drive and AI setup rows. Sign in to CountyRecords in the Chrome window TitleDesk opens.
  3. Paste the assignment and add documents you already have. Click Read assignment and review the extracted facts.
  4. Choose your research settings and purchase ceiling, then click Run AI research.
  5. Review the source-linked runsheet, chain, ownership, net mineral acres and questions. Open the report package and deliver it to your selected Drive folder.
