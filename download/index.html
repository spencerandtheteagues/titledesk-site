Verified installation

Get the right TitleDesk build.

Production installers are currently delivered through assisted onboarding. This keeps customers on a verified build while Windows and macOS signing are completed.

After checkout

  1. Open the TitleDesk activation email and keep its one-time activation link private.
  2. Email support from the same address used at checkout and tell us whether you use Windows, macOS, or Linux.
  3. Install only the build supplied through the verified onboarding reply, then open the activation link on that computer.
