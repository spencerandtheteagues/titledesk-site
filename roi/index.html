Landman title work automation · the arithmetic

Measure the work. Then calculate the return.

A reliable typical assignment time has not been established. County coverage, document volume, scan quality, AI availability and human review all affect elapsed time. Measure completed work on representative assignments before planning capacity or savings.

What is established today

Saved-document comparisons and automated component tests support specific parts of the workflow. They do not establish a typical time for a new assignment from intake through final review and delivery. Current live validation is incomplete. We have removed the earlier one-hour, throughput and savings estimates.

Measure a representative pilot

  1. Select assignments from the counties, tract sizes, historical periods and scan qualities your team handles. Include difficult and incomplete files.
  2. Record the build, hardware, provider, purchase ceiling, documents already held and documents supplied during the run.
  3. Measure elapsed time from Start through a validated package. Record AI waits, vendor interruptions, uploads and stops separately.
  4. Measure the landman’s active time for intake, assistance, checking, corrections and final review.
  5. Check the resulting owners, shares, acres, citations and open items against the source records and an independent review. Include failed and incomplete runs in the results.

Calculate cost from your results

MeasureCalculation
Baseline costMeasured manual labor cost + records and other direct costs.
Cost using TitleDeskMeasured setup, assistance, review and correction labor + allocated software cost + records + AI charges + other direct costs.
Net savingsBaseline cost minus cost using TitleDesk, for equivalent accepted work.
Completion rateAccepted completed assignments divided by all attempted assignments.

Report sample size and a range of observed times, together with completion rate. Keep elapsed run time separate from human time. An interrupted run is not a completed timing observation, and a saved-data replay is not a new live assignment.

Plan capacity after acceptance

Use the actual review standard and correction burden your team requires. Exact fractions and a formatted workbook do not establish that every required instrument was found or interpreted correctly. We do not currently publish a verified savings percentage, weekly output target or payback period.

Published licensing options can be included in your calculation. Confirm county-records and AI charges separately.
