Spend more of your day examining title

TitleDesk brings the county research, recorded instruments, runsheet, chain, exact ownership fractions and net mineral acres together. Saved documents can be reused, local OCR reads scans on your computer, and your client workbook and submission package stay connected to the examination.

Make the estimate your own

Try a representative assignment, then enter your measured time for research, examination, review and delivery. Use a loaded staff rate or the value of your own time. Time freed can create room for another assignment, more careful review or a shorter workday; the calculator expresses that time in dollars.

The Individual plan is $199 per month after a seven-day trial. Use your existing ChatGPT/Codex or Claude plan, or choose an API provider. Include any additional AI usage and research-provider charges in the calculator so your estimate reflects your workflow. Compare plans.
