What AI does in the title workflow

An AI provider can help read page images, propose extracted parties and interests, and organize information for review. TitleDesk keeps that interpretation connected to the assignment, the source records and the report being prepared. A proposed reading still needs the landman's examination.

The integrated workflow also includes steps that do not depend on a model: local document OCR, project records, deterministic fraction calculations and report assembly. This distinction matters when evaluating accuracy, privacy, connectivity and cost.

Local OCR and selected AI examination are separate

  • Local OCR: held PDF and multi-page TIFF scans are processed on the computer without a per-page cloud OCR charge.
  • Automated page examination: selected page images are sent to the AI provider you chose after consent. Provider availability, capability and charges remain part of the workflow.
  • Ownership math: exact fractions are calculated from explicit inputs. Correct arithmetic does not establish the legal meaning of a conveyance.
  • Research connections: county-record providers and Drive receive the information required for their configured tasks.

Read the security and privacy details before choosing a setup. Existing local work and automated connected research have different internet requirements.

Keep the assignment connected from records to reports

TitleDesk's landman software workflow brings the county packet, source-linked runsheet, tract ownership, net mineral acres, curative list and client deliverable into one desktop workspace. Company profiles configure report columns and filing conventions. The installed release and provider coverage determine which connected sources are available for your assignment.

A practical AI landman software demonstration

  1. Use a permitted packet that includes a difficult scan and a missing prior reference.
  2. Open the actual page behind a proposed party, date and fraction.
  3. Correct a reading and check the accepted result in the runsheet and exported report.
  4. Inspect the calculation inputs and the exact ownership total.
  5. Identify what remains unresolved and what further instrument is needed.
  6. Review the client workbook and source folder as separate delivered files.

Measure draft preparation, corrections, unresolved evidence and reviewed completion separately. AI output that looks complete is not itself a finished title assignment.

Can AI replace a landman?

TitleDesk assists research and preparation. A qualified professional still reviews the instruments, ownership interpretation, curative issues and final client package. The workflow should make that review easier by preserving its sources and open questions.
