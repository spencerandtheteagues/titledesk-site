What TitleDesk does

TitleDesk prepares a draft examination of Texas mineral title from the documents supplied for a run. It covers Texas only, and each run covers one county. A run works like this:

  • You sign in with a one-time link that we email to you, or with your Google account. There is no TitleDesk password.
  • You enter the assignment: the county, the tracts with their abstracts and gross acres, the assignment text as you received it, and the names to research.
  • You upload recorded instruments as PDF, TIFF, PNG or JPEG files. For Hybrid and Full service runs, we also buy record copies for you.
  • Before you pay, a free check compares the assignment text with the tracts and acreage you entered.
  • You confirm the assignment and pay, and the run starts.
  • Two AI model sessions, using Google’s Gemini models, examine the documents. The first builds the chain of title and the mineral ownership in exact fractions. The second checks that work, resolves problems and writes the workbook.
  • The workbook has four sheets: Summary, Ownership, Runsheet and Title Review. We email it to you as an attachment, and it is kept in your account.

The workbook is a draft for professional review

A TitleDesk workbook is a draft examination for review by a qualified professional. It is not a title opinion, not an abstract of title and not a survey. We are not a law firm, and TitleDesk does not give legal advice.

A workbook reflects only the documents supplied for the run. It does not account for anything outside them, such as instruments that were not supplied, unrecorded interests, records kept in other counties or offices, or facts that only a survey or an inspection of the land would show.

AI can make mistakes. It can misread a document, miss an instrument, misunderstand a conveyance or get a fraction wrong, and a run can be no better than the documents it was given.

You are responsible for reviewing every workbook, checking it against the source records and deciding whether to rely on it. Before you or anyone else relies on a workbook, for example to buy, sell or lease an interest or to pay an owner, have it reviewed by a qualified professional.

The landman and certification lines in the workbook are left blank for you. If you sign a workbook, you sign it as your own work, after your own review. Do not present a workbook, or let anyone else present it, as a title opinion, an abstract of title, a survey or work certified by us.

Plans

A plan gives you a set number of title examinations each month and a number of seats. The allowance covers Upload, Hybrid and Full service runs; retrieval remains by invitation. Runs beyond the allowance are charged at the plan’s extra-run price. Solo and Pro add a $49 retrieval fee per retrieval run; Enterprise has no retrieval fee.

Each plan offers two document billing options at the same subscription price. With copies at cost, TitleDesk charges the requesting customer's saved card the actual vendor copy cost within that customer's approved per-run limit. With included copies, TitleDesk pays the vendor copy cost within hard per-run and monthly caps and charges the requesting customer $1 per successfully purchased recorded instrument. A multi-page instrument counts once. Duplicates and unsuccessful purchases do not earn a service fee. The included caps are Solo: $25 per run and $100 per month; Pro: $60 per run and $400 per month; Enterprise: $125 per run and $1,000 per month. Monthly caps are shared by every seat on a plan, including extra runs. Neither runs nor copy allowances carry over.

Included copy purchasing stops at either cap; it does not switch automatically to charging the customer the copy cost. Every customer authorizes their own card charge limit for each run. Team membership does not authorize charges to the plan owner's card. The $60 and $125 copy allowances for individually purchased Hybrid and Full service runs remain separate from subscription allowances.
