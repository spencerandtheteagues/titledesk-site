The desktop app

What Desktop Agent does on your machine.

Runs on your computer

Installers for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux as an AppImage. Your work is kept in an encrypted workspace with an append-only audit log.

Local OCR

Scanned records are read on your own computer with no per-page OCR fee, and the documents you already own are reused instead of being bought again.

Your AI provider

Automated document examination uses the AI account you connect. Your Desktop Agent licence, your AI plan and your research-provider purchases each stay under your control.

County research

It researches county indexes by abstract, party and recording reference, and brings recorded instruments into the examination under the purchase ceiling you choose.

Exact fractions

Reservations, probate and heirship questions, ownership fractions and net mineral acres are carried through to the report without rounding the fractions.

Licensed per computer

A seat is one licensed computer. Individuals can start with a 7-day free trial; companies buy seats or ask for a 14-day Enterprise demo. Prices are on the desktop site.

Desktop Agent assists title research. A qualified landman or attorney must review sources, ownership and curatives before client use.
