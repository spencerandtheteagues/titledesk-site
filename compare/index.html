When to put TitleDesk on your shortlist

Choose a TitleDesk evaluation when your main deliverable is a title package: examined records, a source-linked runsheet, ownership and acreage calculations, curative exceptions and reports in the client's format. Its breadth is the connection between those tasks in one desktop workspace.

  • Document-heavy title research: local OCR for held scans, with a separate selected AI provider for automated page examination.
  • Ownership calculations: exact fractions with source evidence beside the proposed interests.
  • Several client formats: company configuration for filing, report columns and deliverable structure.
  • A reviewable handoff: research, exceptions and package preparation kept with the assignment.

If lease administration, enterprise mapping or production and financial reconciliation drive your purchase, include the products designed around those workflows in your evaluation. A team may use a title workspace alongside a land administration platform.

A fair demonstration for every vendor

Give each vendor the same permitted sample packet and blank client template. Keep a manual answer key for the facts you have already checked. Ask the vendor to show these steps rather than relying on a feature checklist:

  1. Open a recorded instrument and identify the page supporting a proposed party, date and interest.
  2. Build a chronological runsheet and flag a deliberately missing prior reference.
  3. Carry stipulated fractional interests through a small example and reconcile them with the tract acreage.
  4. Correct a proposed fact and show how that correction reaches the report.
  5. Export your workbook and folder package, then inspect the actual files.
  6. Interrupt the session, resume it, and identify unfinished research and exceptions.

Track elapsed time, manual corrections, unresolved sources and the final deliverable separately. A fast draft and a reviewed assignment are different milestones.
