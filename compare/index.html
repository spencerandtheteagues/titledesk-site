TitleDesk’s focus

TitleDesk brings local document processing, controlled record purchases and a reviewable ownership workbook into the landman’s desktop workflow. Proposed owners, missing instruments and unresolved title gaps remain visible. This combination is the product’s focus; it is not a claim that these capabilities are exclusive.

Connection and cost boundaries

Local OCR does not use a cloud OCR service. Automated examination uses the compatible AI provider you connect and can consume its quota or credits. CountyRecords is the implemented records adapter; CourthouseDirect and TexasFile integrations are not available yet. Coverage and vendor charges vary.

A fair comparison

Run the same representative evidence through each shortlisted product. Compare accepted owners and shares, missing or incorrect citations, gaps found, human corrections, total elapsed time and total cost. Include unsuccessful attempts. No head-to-head accuracy, speed, security or cost advantage for TitleDesk has been established.
